# LoanWise AI — Design Direction

## Theme Name

**Midnight Signal** — an editorial fintech control room that makes complex loan decisions feel calm, transparent, and human.

## Design movement

Dark glassmorphism with restrained neon signal accents. The interface feels like a private financial cockpit: deep navy space, translucent panels, clear data hierarchy, and small cyan-to-violet moments that reward progress.

## Core principles

- **Clarity over theatrics:** calculations and caveats stay visible.
- **Trust through transparency:** show the rule, the range, and the reason.
- **Progressive confidence:** lead users from a question to a useful next step.
- **Human pace:** motion supports orientation and feedback, never distraction.
- **Indian by default:** ₹, lakhs/crores formatting, Indian phone validation, and approachable wording.

## Color philosophy

- Canvas: midnight navy, ink black, and indigo gradients.
- Primary signal: electric cyan `#6ee7ff`.
- Secondary signal: orchid violet `#9b8cff`.
- Positive: emerald `#56e0ad`.
- Caution: warm amber `#ffc66d`.
- Risk: coral `#ff7c94`.
- Text: soft white with muted blue-gray supporting copy.

## Layout paradigm

Wide, calm editorial compositions with a two-column tool layout on desktop: form and explanation on the left, result cockpit on the right. Single-column flow on mobile. Cards use generous 20–28px radii and layered translucency.

## Signature elements

- Ambient blurred orbs in purple, cyan, and emerald.
- A shield-and-signal logo mark.
- “Signal cards” for score, FOIR, risk, and next-best action.
- Thin glowing divider lines and rounded segmented controls.
- A compact top eyebrow that says what the tool estimates before the headline.

## Interaction philosophy

Every action gets a visible response: field states, subtle lift, ripple-like highlight, skeleton, toast, or result transition. Never hide why a result changed. Forms are forgiving but explicit; the user sees what is missing and how it matters.

## Animation

Fast fade/slide entrance (180–420ms), score rings that ease from zero, number counters, soft hover lifts, and a typing indicator for AI. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling transforms and transition-heavy effects.

## Typography system

Use `Inter` for interface text and `Space Grotesk` for headlines and prominent numerals. Large display type is tight and editorial; body copy is 15–16px with generous line height.

## Brand essence

LoanWise AI is the calm second opinion before a major financial decision.

## Brand voice

Practical, direct, reassuring without overpromising. Prefer “estimated”, “indicative”, “you may improve this by…” and “here’s what affects the result.” Never imply lender approval.

## Wordmark and logo

The wordmark is `LoanWise` with `AI` as a small signal badge. The standalone mark is a filled shield with a rising signal cutout: a visual metaphor for protection plus financial momentum. It is intentionally simple enough to read at 24px.

## Signature brand color

Electric cyan `#6ee7ff`, balanced by orchid violet `#9b8cff`.
