Privacy, in plain language

You stay in control of your numbers.

LoanWise AI is designed to minimize what it asks for and make every optional data flow visible.

What we collect

Only the fields you choose to enter into a calculator or analyzer. We do not request PAN, Aadhaar, OTPs, bank passwords, or account credentials.

How consent works

Saving a record and using a saved profile as AI context are separate choices. You can use the tools without enabling either.

Where data goes

Without configured credentials, saved records stay in your browser's local storage for the current session. With a configured Google Sheets endpoint, the opted-in record is sent through a server-side proxy.

Delete or export

Use My Records to export a CSV or delete browser-session records. Remote deletion depends on the configured storage connector and is never represented as complete unless the connector confirms it.

AI context

Claude receives only the prompt and context you explicitly allow. The server prompt prevents requests for sensitive identity credentials and requires an educational disclaimer.
